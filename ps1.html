Playstation 1 generative sketches

This is in-progress work (as of May 2026) - using the original ps1 development libraries (PSYQ), I'm making some generative sketches that are burned to CDs and run on a real playstation 1. Right now it's mostly flower scenes and landscapes. A lot of people don't like PSYQ, but I've really been enjoying it, working in a winxp virtual machine is an incredible break compared to modern windows.

The ps1 is very very interesting visually, there are a lot of visual artefacts that I want to explore and make work about. A lot of the "playstation 1 styled" games/work I see don't really capture what I think is interesting/beautiful about the system, and end up focusing too much on vertex wobble or keeping a super low polycount. The ps1 has some 3d issues but it's really capable of making beautiful and realistic graphics (ridge racer type 4!). I really just want to try and experiment and see what happens, and hopefully once I get a bit better at woodworking, make some video sculpture based on this work.


Here is one of the sketches running on a real console, it's much slower than running on an emulator, but I think it looks better the slower it is. I'm using a smaller (16 inch diagonal) 16:9 CRT on its side to show these. Until I can make a new case for the tube+chassis, I need to have some magnets around it since there's discoloration when it's placed on its side. I also would really love to RGB mod this screen, hopefully that can happen soon.

More recently (Nov. 2026), I've been trying to include birds/animal behavior in these sketches, to the right is a video of a Mullein / Goldfinch scene that I'm working on right now. It's a rough draft. If you click it, you will be able to hear the sounds that the bird makes.

Yesterday I got a PS1 "Link Cable" that allows you to hook two consoles together, originally this was intended for multiplayer games, where you could have people playing on two consoles in the same room in the same match (I think Twisted Metal III supported up to 8 players using this cable and two multitaps). I really want to use this to make diptychs, whether that's two vertical screens that birds can fly between, or a landscape that spans two horizontal screens.

The idea for having wooden cases that 'surround' the CRT tube itself has changed a bit, for other non-playstation work I'm pursuing this, but for the playstation I think it's important to keep the image on a TV. I think that the woodworking I will do for the ps1 will just be a wooden 'base' to keep the TVs standing up sideways for scenes that require a vertical display, similar to the wooden scholar stones / viewing stones.
